Settings & Rules

Opponent level, match format and team are chosen in the main menu before you press Play.

Table tennis rules

  • Game: first to 11 points, and you must lead by 2. At 10–10 play continues until someone leads by 2.
  • Match: best of 1, 3 or 5 games. The first server alternates each game.
  • Serving: the server changes every 2 points, and every point after 10–10. Only the server holds the ball.
  • Serve: toss the ball up and hit it as it falls. It must bounce on your side first, then on the receiver’s side. If it touches the net it is a let and is replayed.
  • Return: let the ball bounce once on your side, then hit it over the net onto the opponent’s side. You cannot hit it before it bounces.
  • You lose the point if the ball bounces twice on your side, you miss it, your shot hits the net or lands off the table, or your serve is wrong.
  • Not simulated yet: changing ends in the deciding game, doubles and the expedite rule.
